---
name: Astana device access
description: Why participant multi-device sign-in is pairing, not email-only login or account recovery
---

Adding a device and recovering a lost identity are distinct actions. Use a temporary code generated by an already signed-in participant to add a laptop session while preserving the phone session. Keep organizer-verified recovery as the deliberate revocation path for lost devices.

**Why:** Participants at Astana on 2026-09-30 were stranded on their phones because re-entering their email was treated as duplicate registration. The organizer selected phone-generated pairing rather than adding an email-delivery service. Granting access merely from a known name/email would allow impersonation.

**How to apply:** Preserve concurrent participant sessions for device pairing. Keep codes single-use and short-lived. Do not make duplicate check-in silently sign in an existing participant, and do not turn ordinary device addition into session revocation.
